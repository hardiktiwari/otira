# Business Development

Help the team find, qualify, and convert SMB clients for forward-deployed AI coworkers.

## Core principle (right now)
We are in **learn & validate** mode. Default to **discovery**, not pitching. Worst case is we
learn and tweak.

## Discovery call structure (~60–75 min)
1. **Frame (5m):** "We're not selling an ERP rebuild. We find the one workflow that saves you
   the most, automate it on top of what you already use, and expand."
2. **Listen & map (30m):** how a real day/order/invoice works (use the discovery questions).
3. **React (15m):** propose the wedge use case + an honest take.
4. **Next step (10m):** a paid, fixed-scope discovery sprint that ends with a working prototype.

## Discovery questions
See [`../../docs/customer-discovery.md`](../../docs/customer-discovery.md) — workflow, systems &
data, people & success.

## Qualifying an SMB (quick rubric)
- **Pain:** is there slow, manual, error-prone work between systems? (More = better.)
- **Systems:** email + an ERP/QBO + a feed or portal? (Connectable via APIs.)
- **Buyer:** owner/operator who feels the pain and can say yes.
- **Value:** can we name the FTE cost replaced *or* the owner hours reclaimed?
- **Warmth:** warm intro (esp. via the wine network) beats cold every time.

## Positioning & objection handling
- **"Why not just ChatGPT?"** It doesn't connect to your live systems, doesn't know your rules,
  can't act, and can't show its work. We do all four.
- **"Our data's a mess."** Data doesn't need to be clean — it needs to exist. We work on it as-is.
- **"Why not Doss / a new ERP?"** No migration; we layer on what you have and leave you self-sufficient.
- **"My business is fine."** Reframe to quality of life: 10–20 hours/week back, not just dollars.

## Pricing conversation
- Anchor to **FTE cost replaced** and **owner hours reclaimed**.
- **Initiation fee** (build) + **retainer/licensing** (manage & expand). Discuss after discovery.

## Outreach
- Lead with a specific, relatable pain for their vertical; offer to *learn*, not to sell.
- Always disclose clearly and keep it human; never overpromise reliability we can't yet ship.
