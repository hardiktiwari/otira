# One-Pager (plain English)

> For a small-business owner who has never heard "MCP" or "AI agent." No jargon. This is the
> #1 team task — refine it together, then tailor per customer. Keep it to one page.

---

## The problem you already feel
Your business runs on email, your accounting/inventory software, and a pile of spreadsheets.
A few people spend their days copying information between those systems — reading an email,
typing an order in, posting an invoice, chasing a number. It's slow, it's easy to get wrong,
and it doesn't scale. Hiring more people is expensive and takes months.

## What we do
We set up a smart assistant that **plugs into the tools you already use** — your email, your
accounting software, your inventory system — and **learns how your business actually works**:
your products, your customers, your pricing, your rules. Then it does the repetitive work for
you and shows you a result to approve.

Think of it as a **new coworker** — except it works 24/7, doesn't make typos, and you only pay
a fraction of what a hire costs.

## What it looks like day to day
- An order email comes in → it reads it, matches the products and customer, checks pricing and
  stock, and prepares the order. You just glance and click **approve**.
- A vendor invoice arrives → it files it correctly and queues the payment for your okay.
- It keeps an eye on the numbers and flags anything unusual before it becomes a problem.

**You stay in control.** It tells you how confident it is, asks before anything important, and
keeps a record of every action — so you can always see exactly what it did and why.

## Why this is different
- **No new system to learn or move to.** It works on top of what you already have.
- **It knows *your* business** — not generic answers like a chatbot.
- **You own it.** We set it up with you, then hand you the keys; your team can extend it.

## What you get
- Fewer hours lost to manual work (and fewer errors).
- The option to grow without adding back-office headcount.
- **Your time back** — often 10–20 hours a week — to spend on the parts of the business you
  actually care about.

## How we work
We sit with you for a few weeks, set it up around one workflow that's costing you the most,
prove it works on your real data, then expand from there. Simple, low-commitment first step.
