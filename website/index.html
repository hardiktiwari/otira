Trust

It prepares work. Your team approves it.

Wine businesses run on trust. Otira is designed around source links, confidence, and human approval before anything important changes.

  • Every draft cites the email, invoice, spreadsheet, or system it came from

ChatGPT does not know your customer pricing, inventory, or approval rules. Dashboards show what happened, but do not turn an order email or invoice into approved work.

FAQ

Questions

Do we have to replace our accounting, ERP, inventory tools, or spreadsheets?

No. Otira layers on top of the tools your team already uses. The goal is less copying and checking, not a painful system migration.

What workflow should we start with?

Start with one workflow that costs hours every week. For most wine importers and distributors, the best first candidate is customer order emails into approved draft orders.

Will Otira create orders or payments without us?

No. The first version is approval-first: Otira prepares the draft, cites the source, flags uncertainty, and waits for your team.

How long does setup take?

We map the workflow, connect the key systems, and prove the first use case on real data before expanding. The goal is weeks, not a multi-quarter transformation.

Is this only for wine?

Wine is the first focus because the workflow is messy and underserved: vintages, allocations, distributors, inventory, and relationship-driven orders. The same pattern can expand later.

Start with one workflow your team does by hand.

We will map the order, invoice, inventory, or reporting process that slows your wine business down and show where an approval-first coworker can help.
