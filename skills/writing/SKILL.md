# Writing & Communication

Make our writing clear, concrete, and outcome-focused.

## When to use
- Plain-English explainers (see [`../../docs/one-pager.md`](../../docs/one-pager.md)).
- Outreach to prospects/advisors; investor updates; landing-page copy; FAQs.

## Rules
1. **Lead with the reader's pain and the outcome**, not our tech.
2. **No internal jargon** in external material — never "MCP" or "forward deployment" to a
   customer. Use the [`glossary`](../../docs/glossary.md): say "we set it up for you, then you
   own it," "an assistant that plugs into your tools," etc.
3. **Concrete over abstract.** Show a day-in-the-life example, not a capability list.
4. **Short.** One idea per paragraph; one page where possible; cut hedging.
5. **Honest.** Don't overpromise reliability we can't ship; name the human-approval safety net.
6. **One clear ask** per piece (a low-commitment next step).

## Audience presets
- **SMB owner (non-technical):** quality-of-life + dollars; plain words; a relatable workflow.
- **Investor:** problem → why now → wedge → market → model → traction → team → ask.
- **Advisor/peer:** candid, specific, what we're trying to learn from them.

## Process
- Draft → cut 20% → read it aloud → ensure the ask is obvious.
- For decks, hand off to `skills/ppt`. For investor narrative, pull from
  [`../../docs/investor-guide.md`](../../docs/investor-guide.md).

## External comms reminder
Anything sent on someone's behalf must be reviewed and approved by them first, and should be
clearly attributed (no impersonation).
