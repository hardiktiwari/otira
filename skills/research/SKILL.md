# Research

Standardize how we research and capture competitive/market intelligence so it compounds.

## When to use
- "What does <company> do and how are we different?"
- Evaluating a new vertical or market segment.
- A URL is shared and we want signal, not fluff.

## Process
1. **Get the primary source.** Fetch the company's own site (offering, ICP, pricing, FAQ).
   Use LinkedIn/news for funding, team size, traction — but treat marketing as marketing.
2. **Answer four questions:**
   - **What** do they actually do? (offering, not slogans)
   - **Who** do they serve? (industry, revenue band, buyer, motion)
   - **How** do they deliver/price? (product vs. service; self-serve vs. forward-deployed)
   - **How are we different / what do we borrow?**
3. **Place them on our map:** *analyze vs. act* × *SMB vs. enterprise*. Where's the open lane?
4. **Capture it** in [`../../docs/competitive-research.md`](../../docs/competitive-research.md)
   (or `industry-research/<vertical>.md` for markets). Date it. Add a TODO if follow-up is needed.

## Output template (per competitor)
```
## <Name> — <one-line positioning>
- What: ...
- ICP: ...
- Model/Funding: ...
- Difference from us: ...
- Pick up: ...
```

## Guardrails
- **Separate fact from inference** — cite the source; mark assumptions.
- Don't overstate threats or copy positioning blindly; extract what's *useful to us*.
- Prefer concrete capabilities and ICP signals over vibes.
