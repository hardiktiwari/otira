# Finance

The numbers: pricing model, unit economics, cap table, runway. Narrative model lives in
[`../docs/business-model.md`](../docs/business-model.md); this folder holds the actual
spreadsheets/figures.

> ⚠️ Keep sensitive financials out of a public repo. If this repo ever goes public, move real
> numbers to a private store and keep only templates here.

## What goes here
- **Pricing & unit economics** — initiation fee, retainer/licensing, cost-to-deliver, gross margin
  per engagement; how many engagements one person can run.
- **Cap table** — ownership, option pool, any SAFEs/notes.
- **Runway / burn** — simple model: cash in, cost out, months of runway.
- **Invoicing** — how we bill clients (initiation + monthly retainer).

## Quick framing (to fill in)
- Anchor price to **FTE cost replaced + owner hours reclaimed** (see `../docs/business-model.md`).
- Short-term: services revenue (initiation fees) funds the build → can reduce/avoid early raise.
- Long-term: margin improves as the reusable connector library + method compound across clients.
