# Industry Research — Toys / Baby Products (Import + Amazon)

**Opportunistic adjacent vertical.** Anchored on a warm lead: **Davis**.

## The lead: Davis
- Friend of the founders (known ~20 yrs). Designs **baby products**, manufactures in **China**,
  imports to the US, sells **direct on Amazon** (strong ratings).
- **~$40–50M revenue, ~6-person family business** (Davis, dad, cousin, sister).
- Deals with **FX, tariffs, importing** — structurally similar to wine (overseas producers,
  exchange, customs) but **D2C via Amazon** instead of three-tier distribution.
- Profile: **financially comfortable** (potential investor too), **conservative spender**,
  partially retired at ~30. **Not AI-focused**; may be a "my business is fine, why do I need it?"
  conversation → treat as **learning + validation**, not a hard pitch.

## Why interesting for us
- **New connector: Amazon** (Seller Central / marketplace insights) — reusable across e-commerce
  importers.
- **Patent-law module idea:** track patents / check whether a new design infringes open patents
  ("is project XYZ infringing?") — a high-value, differentiated tool for product designers.
- FX / tariff / landed-cost logic overlaps with wine → connector & context reuse.

## Value prop angle
- Even with no layoffs, the pitch lands as **"get 10–20 hours/week of your life back."** For an
  owner like Davis who values time over squeezing net earnings, that's compelling.

## Caveats
- Single data point; not a committed vertical. Use Davis to **learn** the e-commerce/import
  flavor and validate the model, and to explore the Amazon + patent angles.
