# Metrics & KPIs

> What we measure to know if we're winning. Keep it small and honest. Fill targets as we learn.

## North-star (candidate)
**Hours of manual work eliminated per client per week** (proxy for delivered value). Translates
directly into the customer's ROI and our expansion potential.

## Funnel / GTM
- Warm conversations held (discovery calls)
- Discovery → paid pilot conversion
- Time-to-first-working-automation (target: weeks, not months)
- Pilot → retainer conversion

## Delivery / product
- Automation accuracy (% actions correct) + auto-approve rate at high confidence
- Reuse %: how much of a new build is config vs. new code (margin driver)
- Time-to-deploy per use case (should fall with each client)

## Business
- Initiation revenue + monthly retainer (MRR)
- Gross margin per engagement
- Engagements per engineer (capacity)
- Logo count + referrals generated (network land-and-expand)

## Customer outcomes (proof points — capture per client)
- Hours saved/week · errors caught/avoided · headcount avoided · owner hours reclaimed
- Use these as case-study numbers (cf. Sapien's "$12M caught / 30+ hrs saved").
