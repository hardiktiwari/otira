# Investor Guide (working draft)

> Early scaffold for how we'd tell the story to investors. Fill in as we get traction.

## The pitch in one line
We build AI coworkers for SMBs via forward-deployed implementations — a reusable context layer
+ connectors on top of the systems they already use — starting in the wine industry.

## Problem
SMBs run on email + an ERP + spreadsheets, with a few people doing slow, error-prone manual
work between systems. They can't hire their way out, generic AI doesn't know their business,
and ERP replacements are slow and disruptive.

## Why now
- AI is finally good enough to do real operational work; the bottleneck is **context +
  connectivity**, not intelligence.
- A wave of funded entrants validates the category (see comps) — but they cluster in
  *analysis* and *enterprise*, leaving *action + SMB + vertical* open.

## Our wedge & differentiation
- **Action, not just analysis** (vs. Sapien/BlackLine).
- **Layer on top, no migration** (vs. Doss / ERP replacement).
- **Reach data outside the ERP** + build a custom context layer (vs. Intuit IES / ERP-native agents).
- **Vertical depth (wine)** where horizontal platforms see only edge cases.

## Market
- Target band: **$10–30M revenue SMBs** (Intuit's own "mid-market" definition) — a real,
  money-backed segment; we start in wine and expand by vertical.
- Buying behavior: firms already spend ~⅓ of an FTE on single point apps; a connected coworker
  that replaces headcount and reclaims owner time is an easy ROI story.

## Business model
- **Initiation fee** (forward-deployed build, ~4–8 weeks) + **retainer/licensing** to maintain
  & expand. Margins improve as the connector library and method compound across clients.

## Traction plan
1. Land 1–2 lighthouse wine clients (Cameron's lead + his own business).
2. Capture quantified outcomes (hours saved, errors caught, headcount avoided).
3. Use the wine network for land-and-expand (referrals compound — cf. Sapien's PE-portfolio motion).
4. Productize the reusable engine; broaden verticals.

## Comparable funding (context)
- **Doss** — ~$73M total (Series B $55M); Intuit Ventures among investors.
- **Sapien** — ~$8.7M seed (General Catalyst, Neo; OpenAI/Google/Stripe/Ramp angels).
- **Varick** — well-funded, small team, forward-deployed service model.

## Team & gaps
- Founders: product/strategy (PMs) + a wine-industry operator (Cameron / domain + prototype).
- **Critical gap:** a strong **AI engineer** — required before automating anything that moves money.

## Key risks
- **Service vs. platform** — replicability is partial; margin & scale story must be honest.
- **Incumbent encroachment** — analyze-first players moving into action; enterprise players moving down-market.
- **Reliability/trust** — automations act on real money; needs engineering rigor + audit + approvals.
- **Founder bandwidth & roles** — clarify Cameron's role and the team's commitment.
