# Customer Discovery

> Who to talk to, what to ask, and how we qualify. Goal right now: **learn & validate**, not pitch.

## Discovery questions (per prospect)

### The workflow
- Walk me through the last [order / invoice / task] that came in by email — step by step.
- How often does this happen, and how many person-hours does it eat?
- When it goes wrong, what does that cost you?
- What counts as a "match" for a product and a customer — how messy are the names?

### Systems & data
- Is there an API into your ERP, or is CSV export the only way in/out?
- How do third-party data feeds arrive, and where does the mapping break?
- Where should this live — inside an existing app, or a new portal?
- Can we get sample documents + system exports (redacted)?

### People & success
- Who needs to *see* vs. *approve* vs. *configure*?
- Who else touches this — bookkeeper, CPA, ops lead?
- If this worked perfectly, what's the next workflow you'd automate?
- What does "success in 90 days" look like to you?
