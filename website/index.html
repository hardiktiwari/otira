FAQ

Questions

Do we have to replace SAP, QuickBooks, Sage, or our spreadsheets?

No. Otira layers on top. Your ERP and models stay the system of record — we connect and sync, not migrate.

Which accounting systems do you support?

SAP, QuickBooks, Sage, NetSuite, Xero, and more — plus Excel, Google Sheets, billing, HRIS, and data warehouse connectors.

Will it post entries without us?

Never on anything material without approval. Otira prepares, cites, and queues — your team signs off before anything hits the ledger.

How is this different from Aleph or other FP&A tools?

Tools like Aleph excel at mid-market FP&A platforms. Otira is forward-deployed for growing teams — hands-on setup, multi-ERP from day one, and a coworker that acts with your sign-off.

What does it cost?

Less than adding another analyst — initiation for setup plus an ongoing retainer. We'll scope it on a demo call.
