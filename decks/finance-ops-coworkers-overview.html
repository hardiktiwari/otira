03 Questions for you, Cameron

Help us find the highest-pain workflow to start with.

This first conversation is about how NTL actually runs day to day — so we pick the one automation that saves you the most, fastest. Here's what we'd love to dig into.

📥

The order workflow

  • Walk us through the last order that came in by email — step by step.
  • How often does this happen, and how many person-hours does it eat?
  • When it goes wrong, what does that cost you?
  • What counts as a "match" for a product and a customer — how messy are the names?
🔌

Systems & data

  • Is there an API into Traverse, or is CSV export the only way in and out?
  • How do the VIP depletion files come in, and where does the mapping break?
  • Should this live inside Echo, or a new portal?
  • Can we get sample order emails + Traverse/VIP exports (redacted)?
🎯

People & success

  • Who needs to see vs. approve vs. configure? (your six roles)
  • Who else touches this — bookkeeper, CPA, ops lead?
  • If this worked perfectly, what's the next workflow you'd automate?
  • What does "success in 90 days" look like to you?
