# Vision, Mission & Principles

## Vision
Every small business runs like it has a team three times its size — without the headcount,
the migrations, or the technical know-how. The operational backbone (the "keel") is invisible,
reliable, and owned by the business.

## Mission
Give SMBs an AI coworker that plugs into the tools they already use, learns how their business
runs, and does the repetitive work — set up hands-on, then handed off so they own it.

## Who we serve (first)
Owner-operators of complex, under-served SMBs (~$10–30M revenue, ~a dozen employees), starting
in **wine import/distribution**. See [`industry-research/`](industry-research/).

## Principles
1. **Service > software.** We sit with you and build for how you actually work — then leave you
   self-sufficient.
2. **Layer on, don't rip out.** We work on top of existing systems; the ERP is a commodity.
3. **Trust is the product.** Confidence + human approval + full audit on every action.
4. **Land narrow, architect broad.** One workflow first, built as a reusable engine.
5. **Honesty over hype.** Name the risks and the gaps; never overpromise reliability.
6. **Own the outcome, hand over the keys.** Clients should not be dependent on us forever.

## What we're NOT
- Not an ERP. Not a generic chatbot. Not a body shop of one-off custom builds.
